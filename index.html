About NavigatorBBS

NavigatorBBS is on a mission to bring modern hobbyists and tinkerers into the world of Amateur Radio, exploring new possibilities and extending the hobby.

Our work centers on practical software, tools, and ideas that make the Amateur Radio experience more approachable, more connected, and more rewarding for people who enjoy building and experimenting.

We believe the hobby grows when curious people are encouraged to explore, create, and share technology in ways that strengthen both the community and the craft.

Projects

The main project for NavigatorBBS is to develop a modern BBS portal designed for Amateur Radio clubs to share activities and information across digital modes, accessible through client terminals.

Project 01

NavigatorBBS Portal

Coming soon — a modern bulletin board system for Amateur Radio clubs to publish announcements, event information, and club activity updates over digital modes and client-terminal access.
